Universal entry: how the system works.

wenevergonnaclose.com is the single private start for Whole Donuts and The Nurtured Chef. It chooses one safe lane without creating a public profile.

1. Complete one private sequence

Choose a base stick figure, a current feeling, a hoped-for feeling, and a high school graduation year. No name or public profile is requested.

2. Confirm and register

Current and future graduation years require an under-college confirmation for The Table. A private email is used only by the registration service to enforce one universal entry; the browser sends no name.

3. Enter one verified side

The registration service issues a short-lived, single-use token. The Table receives the youth lane; The Counter receives the adult lane. Each destination must validate that token before admitting a guest.

4. Leave through the right e-store

Every destination closes through its own e-store. Buying is optional: guests may take a free item and pass something useful to at least one other person.

For professionals

This guide describes the implemented entry contract, not a credential or a 14-level curriculum. The public professional materials are the Whole Donuts guide, the system contract, and the deployment contract. No path is publicly described as live until its owner verifies it.

Privacy and safety

The public page has no name field, public profile, analytics, image capture, location collection, or public sequence history. The registration API stores only server-keyed hashes of the email and source IP, an anonymous stick-figure sequence, and a short-lived token. It is unavailable unless its D1 binding and secret are configured.
