Universal entry: how the system works.

wenevergonnaclose.com is the single private browser-only start for Whole Donuts and The Nurtured Chef. It chooses one first-party lane without creating a public profile.

1. Complete one private sequence

Choose a base stick figure, a current feeling, a hoped-for feeling, and a high school graduation year. No name or public profile is requested.

2. Confirm your lane

Current and future graduation years require an under-college confirmation for The Table. The browser does not collect an email address or create an account.

3. Enter one first-party side

The Table is the Whole Donuts side and The Counter is the Nurtured Chef side. They are static first-party pages selected by the private browser sequence.

4. Take a free next step

Every side offers a free next step and a pass-it-on prompt. An e-store handoff appears only after a destination owner verifies its route and terms.

For professionals

This guide describes the implemented entry contract, not a credential or a 14-level curriculum. The public professional materials are the Whole Donuts guide, the system contract, and the deployment contract. No path is publicly described as live until its owner verifies it.

Privacy and safety

The public page has no name field, email field, account, analytics, image capture, location collection, or sequence history. It is static, so it cannot enforce one entry per IP or email, verify a mailbox, issue a token, or block direct access to a page. It does not claim that it can.

+U crumbs

A crumb is a voluntary contribution of +U Flava. The program grows through each person's touch.

Only a future human-reviewed process may gather, consolidate, arrange, integrate, and record crumbs. The current public site collects and records no crumbs.
