## Privacy and safety boundary

The public entry does not collect names, email addresses, photos, exact age or
birth date, location, accounts, payment details, or analytics. It does not
create a public profile, visit record, return pass, or sequence history.

Because this is a static site, it cannot enforce one entry per IP address or
email address, prove email ownership, issue a single-use token, or block
direct access to a page. Do not claim those features until a separate,
reviewed server-side service is live.

Do not add tracking, profiling, network scanning, automatic content intake,
automatic publication, or automatic merges. A future capability is public only
when it is implemented, reviewed, and truthfully labeled.

## Publication checklist

Before adding an external destination or e-store link, confirm its ownership,
availability, privacy terms, safety, and free-item experience. Update this
system map, the guide, the release notes, and the routing manifest in the same
reviewed change.
